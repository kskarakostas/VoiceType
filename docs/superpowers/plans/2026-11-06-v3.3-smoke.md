# VoiceType 3.3 smoke checklist (for K. S. Karakostas)

Branch `v3.3`, version still 3.2.0: the bump to 3.3.0 comes after section 1 passes. Covers what 3.3 added or changed: Minimal beside the field and in four sizes, five recording colours, four sound sets. Then one list of every manual row still open from earlier lists. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-v3.3-design.md`.

The Run column says who has it: **H** a harness ran it (result in the row), **M** a person has to. Your part is every M row. Fill Result with pass, fail (with the exact on-screen text or what you heard) or n/a. Needs a working key, a real microphone and real speakers.

Order (Task 7 of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-06-v3.3.md`): Atlas hands you the rows one at a time, the gate rows first (section 1, rows 1.1 to 1.3, the 3.0 rows 6.8, 6.9 and 6.10 with Soft), then sections 2 to 5 in order, and stops until each M row has a result. If 1.1 or 1.2 fails, V63 applies: Atlas shortens and then quietens Soft's start, and you repeat the row. The version bump waits for the gate.

Build, then reload VoiceType on `chrome://extensions` and reload any open page:

```bash
npm run build
```

Fixture pages, in a second terminal:

```bash
node test/fixtures/serve.mjs
```

`http://localhost:8765/fields.html` has a text field `#text`, a textarea `#ta`, a same-origin frame and a cross-origin frame, and a textarea `#bottom` fixed at the bottom of the window. The dictation hotkey is Ctrl+Shift+Space. The settings are under Settings, Appearance and Settings, Sound and clipboard. For light and dark, switch the OS, or Chrome's own Appearance setting, between the two and reload the page.
