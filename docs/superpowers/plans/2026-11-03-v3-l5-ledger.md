- Final: a recording in a subframe had no indicator anywhere when the page hid or covered the frame without moving it (`visibility`, `opacity`, an opaque overlay), found by the final review. Fixed with `trackVisibility` and `delay: 100`. Reverses the plan's deferral of `visibility: hidden`. Cost if wrong: a mirror beside a visible indicator.
- Final: a cross-origin frame counts as covered when anything overlaps any part of it, the mirror included. So a frame near the window bottom or under a fixed header keeps a second indicator up. Accepted: the safe side of the anti-goal; stop works, nothing is lost.
- Final: `opacity: 0.99` or a rotate on an iframe shows the mirror beside a visible indicator. Accepted.
- Final: a held status on Minimal stays at the frame's bottom centre, since its field lost focus. Inside Q3. Cost if wrong: a tall frame hides it; the result is not lost.
- Final: extension disabled with the mirror up and a press inside the frame leaves the mirror up to 75 s, as frame 0 is not told. Accepted: a shorter timer risks hiding a live recording.
- Smoke: observe a 1 px point at the indicator's centre, not its box, so a page edge shadow or our own menu or status shadow is no cover. Cost if wrong: an opaque overlay that leaves the centre clear does not count as cover (run by the re-review).
- The first diagnosis of the TinyMCE case (header shadow) was wrong in the ruling that led to 9929198: it is the focus ring. The sight point stays for what it does fix. Found by the re-review and by the probe.
