## Step A: word segmentation (V15)

Node v20.20.2, ICU 78.2, `new Intl.Segmenter(undefined, { granularity: 'word' })`, counting `isWordLike` segments. Pinned in `test/unit/platform/segmenter.test.js` (6/6, commit c9805b5):
- English: `Hello, world! It's a test.` gives 5; `don’t won't` gives 2 (contractions whole, both apostrophes).
- Greek: `Καλημέρα, τι κάνεις; Είμαι καλά.` gives 5 (accents, final sigma, the Greek question mark `;` skipped).
- Mixed: `Το API του OpenAI κοστίζει 0,006 $ ανά λεπτό.` gives 8 (a number is one word; `$` is not).
- Empty, blank and punctuation-only text give 0; newlines split.
- Accepted edge cases: hyphenated words split (`e-mail` gives 2); the Greek `ό,τι` gives 2.
