## Deferred

Later or never:
- Hostile page only: the forgeable `voicetype:teardown` event; `mask` on an iframe (reads as visible); `beforetoggle` `preventDefault` or `removeAttribute('popover')` under a modal; `html { content-visibility: hidden }` under a modal; a held view transition. A page that hides itself is out of scope.
- `document.open()` on the recording page: the indicator comes back, but its listeners do not, so the hotkey cannot stop it; it ends at max time or tab close.
- A top page that never runs our script (a `file:` page without file access): no mirror, in the spec under V19.
- A tab switch away leaves no indicator in the other tab; Chrome's own system tray indicator stays. The anti-goal says "on the page".
- A worker crash during a recording leaves the frame showing recording with the mic on; needs a crash, not idle (read, not run).
- A worker killed during processing over 30 s: the transcript is lost, and "No response" shows at 75 s. Not measured; same as 2.2. TO CONFIRM with a 45 s provider on real Chrome with no DevTools.
- A zoom change during a recording leaves Dot and Pill at the old field box until the next scroll or resize, on screen. A px-sized field fires no watcher.
- `DICTATION_RESULT` dropped during starting or recording: needs a press crossing the result, one IPC.
- A failed menu save or an orphan notice replaces a click-to-copy status.
- A chord refused during a landing and still held does not record when the landing ends.
- `dictate.js` awaits the usage write with no limit: needs `chrome.storage` to hang.
- `finish` ignores a `{ ok: false }` offscreen reply: the session stays processing until the worker idles.
- Rollback merge: keywords go into `any` first, so at the 100 cap other groups lose terms, and a keyword equal to a term of another group moves it to `any`.
- Two pages saving `modes` or `dictionary` inside one re-read round trip can lose an edit (spec V18 says so).
- `settingsVersion: 2.5` takes the v1 path (hand edit only).
- A blank mode name blocks the editor's save, with a toast.
- CSV export writes `=` terms as is; a 2.2 term over 64 characters makes an export of it fail on import into an empty dictionary; `sep=;` first lines are read as terms.
- `modes` with custom prompts go to every frame, unused there (pre-existing).
- `pill.js` is past 800 lines; the sight block and the raise could move out.
- The offscreen listener's check is `sender.tab`, not "the service worker": only the worker sends those names.
- Tests: `pill-styles.spec.js:830` rapid flips assert `removed > 0` on 200 ms beats (not seen flaky); `pill.test.js:1841` passes with or without `?.`; no test orphans mid-landing; the landing guard is unit tests only; a `copyText` throw is the only test of its `finally`.
- L6 task log: the harnesses for L1 to L3 ran on Chromium only.
