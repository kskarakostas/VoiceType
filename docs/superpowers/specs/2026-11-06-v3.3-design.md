# VoiceType 3.3 Design Spec

Prepared 2026-11-06 for K. S. Karakostas. Extends `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-v3.2-design.md` and the specs it names, whose decisions V1 to V57, anti-goals and global constraints stay in force except where this spec says otherwise. Sources: the code at `06427bd` (v3.2.0) and the comparison panel K. S. Karakostas reviewed on 2026-11-06.

Tags: **VERIFIED** (read in the code or measured), **DECIDED** (K. S. Karakostas, 2026-11-06), **PROPOSED** (Atlas's call, changed only with K. S. Karakostas's approval).

## 1. Intent

K. S. Karakostas uses Minimal and likes it, but the indicator sits at the bottom of the page, away from where he writes; the wave is too small to enjoy; and the sounds are aggressive. 3.3 is a look and feel release: Minimal beside the field and bigger, softer sounds, and a choice of size, sound set and recording colour.

**Goals**
- Minimal shows next to the field, as Pill does.
- Minimal comes in four sizes, Medium by default, with a wave large enough to read.
- Four sound sets, Soft by default, each with a way to hear it before choosing.
- Five recording colours, Classic red by default, readable in light and dark.

**Non-goals:** a free colour picker; colouring menus, the quick panel or the settings page; a theme; an option to keep Minimal at the bottom; a volume control; new sound files.

**Anti-goals** (3.3 fails if any of these happens): the 3.0, 3.1 and 3.2 lists, and:
- A start sound reaches a transcript.
- A status colour follows the palette: an error stays red and a success stays green whatever is chosen.
- A preset fails the contrast limits of V60 in light or dark.
- Minimal takes a click, or covers the text being written.
- Dictation or an edit waits on a sound, a size or a colour.
- A new permission, a new runtime dependency or an audio file.

## 2. Decisions

| # | Decision | Choice | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| V58 | Minimal position | Replaces V19 for a field in view. With a field in view, Minimal uses the placement of `computePillPosition` with its own size: left of the field, else right, else above, else below. Minimal never takes the `inside` slot, which would cover text. With no outside slot, no field, or the field out of sight, it uses `computeIndicatorPosition`, bottom centre, not Pill's corner. It stays `pointer-events: none`. A status follows the indicator's own box whenever the indicator is beside a field, in every frame; at the bottom centre the rules of today stay | DECIDED, the fallback PROPOSED |
| V59 | Minimal size | `miniSize`: `small`, `medium` (default), `large`, `xlarge`, on Settings, Appearance, shown only while Minimal is the chosen style. Sizes are in table 3. `MINI_SIZE` becomes a lookup by size, and `.mini` follows by a data attribute on `.vt`. Bar heights in table 3 are the full height. Every bar keeps today's `scaleY(0.3 + level * gain)`, and more bars repeat today's four gains, 0.5, 0.7, 0.6, 0.4, in order | DECIDED |
| V60 | Recording colour | `recColor`: `red` (default), `vampire`, `obsidian`, `emerald`, `mocha`, on Settings, Appearance, as named swatches. Values are in table 4. It changes the three tokens `--vt-rec`, `--vt-rec-hover` and `--vt-glow` on `.vt`, in all three styles and the mirror, and nothing else. Limits, on the base colour: at least 4.5:1 with white, the REC timer's text colour, and at least 3:1 on both pill surfaces, `#ffffff` and `#1f1f1f`, for the bars. A unit test computes both | DECIDED, limits and names PROPOSED |
| V61 | Sound sets | `soundSet`: `soft` (default), `warm`, `tap`, `classic`, on Settings, Sound and clipboard, beside the sounds switch and not on Appearance. Values are in table 5. Sounds stay off by default. Each set has one play button that plays start, stop, done and error in turn, at any setting of the switch; the page plays them itself by `createCuePlayer`, no message to the worker | DECIDED, the page and the button PROPOSED |
| V62 | Which cue plays | `cue` in `recorder.js` sends `soundSet` with `OFFSCREEN_CUE`, read at the session's start with `sounds`, so one recording has one set. A recovered dictation (V51) takes the stored record's value. An unknown value plays Soft | PROPOSED |
| V63 | Start sound in the microphone | The start cue plays with the microphone live (VERIFIED, `recorder.js`), and a Soft start outlasts the 250 ms a unit test holds today. The test's limit for a whole cue, tail included, moves to 450 ms for start and 700 ms for the others. Smoke rows 6.8 and 6.9 of the 3.0 list, never run, become a gate for 3.3, run before the version bump: real speakers and a real microphone with Soft. If the start sound reaches a transcript, shorten and then quieten Soft's start in table 5 until both rows pass, and leave its other cues alone. Finishing the cue before the microphone opens stays ruled out | PROPOSED |
| V64 | Hide pill and the mirror | A site with Hide pill keeps the Small indicator at the bottom centre, as the page was asked to stay clear; so does the mirror, which stands in for a frame out of sight and has no field of its own. Both take the recording colour | PROPOSED |
| V65 | Settings | Three new known fields in `DEFAULT_SETTINGS`. `settingsVersion` stays 3: `validateV3` repairs a missing or unknown value to the default, as it did for `editHotkey` (VERIFIED, `upgrade.mjs`). `miniSize` and `recColor` are public, content scripts need them. `soundSet` goes in `PRIVATE_FIELDS`, no content code reads it. Reset to defaults resets all three | PROPOSED |
| V66 | Existing users | A 3.2 user on Minimal moves beside the field at Medium. A 3.2 user with sounds on hears Soft. Both are the point of the release; the CHANGELOG says so, and Classic and Small bring back the old sound and size | DECIDED |
| V67 | Previews and copy | The Minimal card on Appearance shows the indicator beside the field and its hint says so. README, Sites and the 3.2 lines that say "at the bottom of the page" change for Minimal only. The preview does not follow the chosen size or colour: the swatches and the live pill show those | PROPOSED |

## 5. Sound sets

Soft, Warm and Single tap as K. S. Karakostas heard them in the panel. Each entry is frequency in Hz and length in ms. Soft, Warm and Single tap: a note starts at 85% of the previous note's length, so notes overlap. Its attack is a linear ramp to the gain, then its decay is exponential from that peak to 0.0008 at 1.5 times the note's length. Warm's filter sits between the oscillator and the gain, with the default Q. Classic stays exactly as 3.2 has it, timing and tests included.

| Value | Label | Wave | Gain | Attack | Filter | Start | Stop | Done | Error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `soft` | Soft | sine | 0.07 | 20 ms | none | 440/110, 587/190 | 587/110, 440/190 | 587/110, 784/240 | 294/160, 247/240 |
| `warm` | Warm | triangle | 0.09 | 15 ms | low-pass 1200 Hz | 392/110, 523/190 | 523/110, 392/190 | 523/110, 659/240 | 262/160, 220/240 |
| `tap` | Single tap | sine | 0.08 | 12 ms | none | 523/200 | 392/200 | 659/260 | 247/280 |
| `classic` | Classic | sine | 0.18 | 5 ms | none | 660/70, 880/90 | 880/70, 660/90 | 880/70, 1320/110 | 330/120, 247/120 |

In the three new sets each oscillator stops at 1.6 times its note's length, so `onended` waits for the tail. `AudioContextLike` gains `createBiquadFilter`. A new context per cue and `CLOSE_TAIL_MS` stay as they are.

## 7. Testing

- Unit: placement with and without a field for each size; a field out of view gives bottom centre; `validateV3` repair for the three fields; `toPublicSettings` leaves out `soundSet`; preset contrast computed from table 4; every set's four cues inside V63's limits; the offscreen fallback.
- e2e on Chromium 153 and Chrome 140: Minimal beside the field at each size, with a click passing through it; Hide pill and the mirror at the bottom in Small; each colour on bars, REC and glow through computed style, a status unchanged; forced colours unchanged; the play button; a 3.2 record reads as Medium, Soft and Classic red.
- Manual, K. S. Karakostas: rows 6.8, 6.9 and 6.10 of the 3.0 list with Soft (a gate, V63); Minimal on Gmail, Slack, Notion and ChatGPT; each colour by eye in light and dark.
